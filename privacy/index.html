Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

The Gear App (gearapp.io) is run by Peter Paton Media (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use The Gear App, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We are the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Questions or requests: contact@peterpaton.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Name, email address and profile picture from Google if you choose “Continue with Google”. We only ask Google for this basic profile information.

What you put into the app

  • Your inventory (the gear you add, quantities and status), groups, and projects with their kit lists and any client name, shoot dates and location you enter.
  • For Suggest, the description of the shoot you type and any changes you ask for. These are sent to our AI provider to build the kit list (see below). We don’t keep the text on our servers; your recent kits are saved in your own browser.

Plan and payments

  • Your plan, Suggest credit balance and credit history, and a record of each Suggest run (when it ran and how much AI processing it used), so we can apply plan limits and track costs.
  • If you pay, our payment provider Stripe collects your card and billing details. We never see or store your full card number; we keep the Stripe customer and subscription references so your plan stays in sync.

When you use the site

  • Usage statistics from Vercel Web Analytics, such as pages viewed, browser, device type and approximate country. It doesn’t use cookies and isn’t used to identify you.
  • Technical data that services we rely on receive when your browser connects to them, such as your IP address (for example our hosting, database and the font and script libraries the site loads).

Why we use it

  • To provide the app and your account: storing your inventory and projects, signing you in, creating PDFs and running Suggest. This is necessary for our contract with you.
  • To take payments and manage your plan: necessary for our contract with you, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to improve it using usage statistics and error information: our legitimate interests in running a reliable service.
  • To contact you about your account, such as sign-up confirmation, password resets and important changes to the service.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions. Your account and app data are stored in the EU (Paris, France).
  • Vercel — website hosting and cookie-free web analytics.
  • Stripe — payments and subscriptions.
  • Anthropic — the AI model behind Suggest. It receives your shoot description, any changes you ask for and relevant items from the equipment catalog, but not your name or email. Anthropic doesn’t use data sent through its API to train its models.
  • Google — if you sign in with Google. Google’s own privacy policy covers your Google account.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or with a buyer if the business is ever sold, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it — inventory, groups, projects, kit lists, plan, credits and Suggest records — are kept until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (on your Account page, under Delete account) permanently removes all of it straight away and cancels any subscription.
  • Stripe keeps records of payments you’ve made, as payment and tax law requires.

Cookies and local storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores some information in your browser’s local storage so it works well: your sign-in session (if you choose “Keep me signed in”), a copy of your inventory, groups and recent Suggest kits for quick loading, and small preferences such as whether you’ve seen the welcome tour. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, and signing out removes your sign-in session.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. You can do much of this yourself in the app, including deleting your account. For anything else, email contact@peterpaton.com and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

The Gear App is for people aged 16 and over. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes we’ll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let you know in the app or by email.
